About the Firm

Zest Engineering Solutions Pvt. Ltd. (ZESPL) is a multidisciplinary engineering consulting firm registered with the Government of Nepal on 12 Chaitra 2079. We provide professional services in engineering and science to private developers and to government.

The firm was established by a group of experienced engineers, investors and planners together with young professionals. We specialise in survey, design, optimisation, project scheduling, contract management and supervision of civil, hydro-mechanical, electro-mechanical and transmission works for hydropower projects.

Objectives

To provide cost-effective, high-quality professional services that meet diverse client needs, with professional ethics, timely performance and total quality assurance. Our experience spans hydropower projects, construction and supervision works, planning and management, and EIA/IEE studies.
